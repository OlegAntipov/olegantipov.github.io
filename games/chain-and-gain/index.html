Hi there! Try this cute casual puzzle game. Your task is to connect matching items into chains — not just horizontally and vertically, but also diagonally! This opens up a world of spectacular moves and unexpected combos.

Every move changes the board: when you clear a chain, blocks from above fall down, and the bottom row disappears. This keeps the gameplay fast and dynamic!

Use awesome power-up items! They are activated automatically if they end up next to your chain, and trigger chain reactions with bright explosive effects! But stay alert: you'll also encounter obstacles that will complicate your task.

Chain them up and gain points — good luck!
